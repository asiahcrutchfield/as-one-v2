# Nia

- Age: 9
- Residence: New Orleans
- Language: English
- Ethnicity: Black
- Nationality: American

## Backstory
Nia is a 9-year-old girl from New Orleans. She has a best friend named Annika [tentative name], who feels almost like a sister to her. They are both Jehovah's Witnesses.

Nia and Annika are inseparable. They do almost everything together: field service, playing, watching videos, and sharing their favorite songs. Their biggest shared interest is the famous Taiwanese pop group Lover Boys [tentative name], who are scheduled to perform at Jazz Fest.

(Annika is more outgoing than Nia and often encourages her to be brave. After Annika’s death, Nia sometimes imagines what Annika would say when she is scared.)

The two girls make plans to see Lover Boys together.

However, Annika dies, leaving Nia devastated. Nia copes by imagining that she will see Annika again in paradise. She owns a stuffed tiger named Sunny, who becomes both her pet and protector in these imagined paradise scenes.

In honor of Annika, Nia becomes determined to see Lover Boys at Jazz Fest for both of them.

# Antoine

- Age: 28
- Residence: New Orleans
- Language: English
- Ethnicity: Black
- Nationality: American
- Playable: Yes

## Backstory
Antoine is a New Orleans resident who has spent years trying to separate himself from the life he grew up around. When he was younger, he was involved with people who moved through the city’s nightlife, street crews, security work, and unofficial business surrounding major events.

Antoine was never the worst person in that world, but he learned how to fight, intimidate, survive, and read danger quickly. After seeing how badly that life could hurt people, he walked away and tried to become someone different.

He now lives quietly and avoids trouble as much as possible. However, the arrival of the Taiwanese pop group Lover Boys [tentative name] pulls him back into old connections he wanted to leave behind. Someone from his past appears to be involved with the threat surrounding the group’s Jazz Fest performance.

Antoine does not want to be a hero. He wants to stay out of it. But when people around him are put in danger, especially innocent people, he cannot ignore what is happening.

Antoine left his old life after someone close to him was hurt because of a decision he made. Since then, he has tried to live quietly and avoid becoming the kind of man people fear.

His gameplay is physical and direct. He fights literal enemies, protects people, and survives violent situations long enough for the truth to come out.

- Ethnicity: Taiwanese
- Nationality: Taiwanese
- Playable: Yes

## Backstory
陳柏安 is a Taiwanese officer assigned to a team investigating a threat connected to the internationally famous Taiwanese pop group Lover Boys [tentative name]. When suspicious activity begins surrounding the group’s trip to New Orleans for Jazz Fest, 柏安 is sent to help uncover who is involved and stop the threat before it reaches the public.

柏安 is calm, observant, and careful. He is not the type to rush into a situation without understanding the full picture first. His strength is seeing patterns, connecting small details, and making tactical decisions under pressure.

At first, the case appears to be a security issue involving the pop group’s international travel. But as 柏安 investigates deeper, he discovers that the threat reaches beyond Taiwan and has connections in New Orleans.

柏安’s investigation eventually leads him toward Antoine’s past and Nia’s world, though he does not understand at first how a former street-level fighter and a grieving child could be connected to the same case.

柏安 is good at controlling situations, but he struggles when a case becomes emotionally messy. He trusts evidence more than people, which can make it difficult for him to believe Nia at first.

His gameplay is tactical and strategic. He gathers information, controls space, identifies suspects, protects civilians, and creates opportunities for the threat to be stopped.
