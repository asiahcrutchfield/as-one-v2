# Player Characters

## Nia

- Age: 9
- Residence: New Orleans
- Language: English
- Ethnicity: Black
- Nationality: American
- Playable: Yes

### Backstory
Nia is a 9-year-old girl from New Orleans. She has a best friend named Annika, who feels almost like a sister to her. They are both Jehovah's Witnesses.

Nia and Annika are inseparable. They do almost everything together: field service, playing, watching videos, and sharing their favorite songs. Their biggest shared interest is the famous Taiwanese pop group HEARTLINE, who are scheduled to perform at Jazz Fest.

(Annika is more outgoing than Nia and often encourages her to be brave. After Annika’s death, Nia sometimes imagines what Annika would say when she is scared.)

The two girls make plans to see HEARTLINE together.

However, Annika is killed by stray gunfire during gang-related violence in the neighborhood, leaving Nia devastated. She was not involved and was not the target. Nia copes by imagining that she will see Annika again in paradise. She owns a stuffed tiger named Sunny, who becomes both her pet and protector in these imagined paradise scenes.

In honor of Annika, Nia becomes determined to see HEARTLINE at Jazz Fest for both of them.

### Gameplay Role
Nia’s gameplay is a puzzle-RPG set inside her imagined paradise. She does not physically fight. Instead, she commands Sunny, her stuffed tiger imagined as a real tiger, to protect her, reveal hidden details, scare away fear, and help her move through emotional obstacles.

Nia’s sections represent real-world challenges: noticing danger, resisting panic, remembering Annika, finding courage, hiding, escaping, or speaking up when adults overlook her.

### Music Mechanic

Nia can use memories of HEARTLINE songs to steady herself when she is afraid.

The songs remind her of Annika, but they do not erase her grief. Instead, they help Nia stay present long enough to notice danger, hide, remember details, or speak.

In gameplay, this can appear as a short rhythm, breathing, or pattern-matching mechanic.

Example uses:

- Hum: lowers panic while Nia is hiding.
- Remember: triggers a memory of Annika that helps Nia process a clue.
- Listen: lets Nia notice a HEARTLINE-related detail adults miss.
- Sunny Guard: allows Sunny to protect Nia from fear, panic, or imagined danger.
- Sunny Sense: reveals hidden details inside Nia’s paradise space.

### Design

## Sunny

- Type: Stuffed tiger / imagined protector
- Connected Character: Nia
- Playable: Yes

### Backstory
Sunny is Nia’s stuffed tiger. Before Annika’s death, Sunny is part of Nia and Annika’s games about paradise.

After Annika dies, Sunny becomes more important to Nia. In her imagined paradise, Sunny is real: a powerful tiger who protects her when the real world feels too frightening.

Sunny does not replace Nia’s courage. He gives shape to it. When Nia commands Sunny, she is finding a way to act even when she feels powerless.

### Gameplay Role
Sunny is central to Nia’s puzzle-RPG gameplay.

Possible commands include:

- Guard: protects Nia from fear, panic, or imagined danger.
- Sense: reveals hidden details in the paradise space.
- Roar: pushes back fear, silence, or pressure.
- Pounce: breaks emotional obstacles or interrupts imagined threats.
- Stay: holds a space, memory, or danger in place while Nia moves.

## Antoine

- Age: 30
- Residence: New Orleans
- Language: English
- Ethnicity: Black
- Nationality: American
- Playable: Yes

### Backstory
Antoine is a New Orleans local, born and raised. He grew up in a rough neighborhood and got involved with dangerous people when he was younger. As he got older, he began trying to turn his life around and distance himself from that world. Antoine’s biggest flaw is that he thinks protecting people means handling everything alone.

His girlfriend knew him during that earlier part of his life, but she believes he can become better than the people he used to run with. She encourages him to stay on the straight and narrow, even when his past keeps reaching for him. She supports Antoine, but she is not willing to save him from himself forever.

Antoine also wants to be a good role model for his niece, Nia, especially because her father is not in the picture. He is protective and supportive of her, though he is not one of Jehovah’s Witnesses and does not fully understand the religious world she grew up in.

When HEARTLINE comes to New Orleans for Jazz Fest, Antoine’s old life pulls him back in. People from his past are hired as local fixers for a job connected to the group’s festival equipment and backstage access.

They approach Antoine because he knows the city, the routes, and the kinds of people who can move through major events without drawing attention. Antoine refuses, but they notice his connection to Nia and threaten that something could happen to her if he does not cooperate.

The situation becomes personal when Antoine realizes Nia is determined to attend HEARTLINE’s performance for Annika. Protecting her means confronting the world he tried to escape.

Now Antoine has to protect the life he is building without becoming the man he used to be. If he wants to cut ties with his old world for good, he may have to cut the cord from the inside.

### Gameplay Role
Antoine’s story is driven by pressure. His old crew tries to pull him back in, and when he refuses, they use Nia as leverage.

At Jazz Fest, Antoine’s flaw reaches a breaking point. He believes he can protect Nia by handling everything alone, but when the danger escalates and Nia slips away, he is forced to rely on others.

Antoine’s gameplay is physical and direct. He fights literal enemies, protects people, and survives dangerous situations while trying not to become the kind of man he used to be.

His biggest gameplay tension is control: he is strong enough to hurt people, but his goal is to protect without losing himself to violence.

## 陳柏安

- Age: 34
- Residence: Taipei, Taiwan
- Language: Mandarin Chinese, English
- Ethnicity: East Asian
- Nationality: Taiwanese
- Playable: Yes

### Backstory
陳柏安 is a Taiwanese police investigator assigned to an international security liaison team connected to HEARTLINE [tentative name], an internationally famous Taiwanese pop group preparing to perform at Jazz Fest in New Orleans.

At first, the case appears to be a travel and event-security concern: suspicious messages, unusual access requests, and signs that someone may be targeting the group’s arrival. 柏安’s job is not to act like a lone hero, but to gather information, connect details, and coordinate with the people responsible for protecting the group.

柏安 is calm, observant, and careful. He does not rush into a situation without understanding the full picture. His strength is seeing patterns, reading small inconsistencies, and making tactical decisions under pressure.

As he investigates, 柏安 realizes the threat is not limited to Taiwan. Someone connected to the group’s New Orleans appearance appears to be working with people on both sides of the trip.

The case eventually leads him toward Antoine’s past and Nia’s world, though he does not understand at first how a former street-level fighter and a grieving child could be connected to the same threat.

柏安 trusts evidence more than emotion. This makes him effective as an investigator, but it also makes it hard for him to believe Nia at first when what she knows comes from memory, fear, and things adults have overlooked.

### Gameplay Role
柏安’s gameplay is tactical and strategic. He gathers information, controls space, identifies suspects, protects civilians, and creates opportunities for the threat to be stopped. When he is outside Taiwan, his role is coordination and intervention, not acting as if he has full police authority in another country.

柏安’s investigation focuses on official systems: travel records, equipment manifests, access badges, security schedules, and suspicious communications.

He can identify patterns and inconsistencies, but he does not immediately understand the local meaning behind them. His evidence points toward New Orleans, but Antoine understands the people behind the local crew, and Nia notices fan-facing details that official records miss.

# Non-player Characters

## Annika

- Age: 9
- Residence: New Orleans
- Language: English
- Ethnicity: Black
- Nationality: American
- Playable: No

### Backstory
Annika is Nia’s best friend and feels almost like a sister to her. She is outgoing, playful, and usually the one who encourages Nia to be brave.

She and Nia are both Jehovah’s Witnesses and spend much of their time together: field service, playing, watching videos, and listening to HEARTLINE. Annika loves HEARTLINE as much as Nia does, and the two girls make plans to see them together at Jazz Fest.

Annika is killed by stray gunfire during gang-related violence in the neighborhood. She was not involved and was not the target.

After her death, Annika remains present in Nia’s memory. When Nia is scared, she sometimes imagines what Annika would say to help her be brave.

### Design

## HEARTLINE

HEARTLINE functions as the emotional and plot center of the story.

For Nia and Annika, HEARTLINE represents friendship, music, and the promise to attend Jazz Fest together.

For Antoine, HEARTLINE’s arrival creates the situation that brings his old life near Nia.

For 柏安, HEARTLINE’s trip is the case that reveals the connection between Taiwan-side corruption and New Orleans local fixers.

HEARTLINE’s songs also appear in Nia’s gameplay as memories that help her stay calm, remember Annika, and notice important details.

## Nia's Mom [tentative name]

- Age: 32-38 [tentative]
- Residence: New Orleans
- Language: English
- Ethnicity: Black
- Nationality: American
- Playable: No
- Relationship: Nia’s mother, Antoine’s sister

### Backstory
Nia’s mother became one of Jehovah’s Witnesses when Nia was an infant. Her faith shapes Nia’s world, especially the belief that Annika can be seen again in paradise.

After Annika’s death, she tries to comfort Nia with the hope of paradise, but she struggles to understand that Nia’s grief is not simple. She wants to protect Nia from more pain, but she cannot fully control what Nia feels or what Nia needs.

She worries about Nia going to Jazz Fest, especially as danger begins surrounding HEARTLINE’s arrival. To her, the festival starts to feel like another place where something could go wrong. To Nia, it feels like the last promise she has left with Annika.

Her relationship with Antoine is complicated. She loves her brother, but she knows parts of his past and does not always trust his judgment. At the same time, she also knows he loves Nia and would do anything to protect her.

### Story Role
Nia’s mother represents the adult world trying to comfort and protect Nia. She also creates emotional tension around whether Nia should be allowed to attend Jazz Fest.

# Antagonists

## Taiwan-side Insider [tentative name]

- Residence: Taiwan
- Language: Mandarin Chinese, English [tentative]
- Nationality: Taiwanese [tentative]
- Playable: No
- Role: Main antagonist / hidden threat source

### Backstory
The Taiwan-side insider is connected to HEARTLINE’s travel, management, security, or equipment logistics.

They are trying to recover or destroy evidence hidden inside HEARTLINE’s festival equipment before the group performs at Jazz Fest. The evidence could expose a criminal arrangement between people in Taiwan and New Orleans.

The insider does not personally handle the New Orleans side. Instead, they work through local fixers who understand Jazz Fest access, service routes, fake credentials, and intimidation.

### Story Role
The Taiwan-side insider creates the international side of the threat. They are the reason 柏安’s investigation begins and the reason HEARTLINE’s trip becomes dangerous.

They should not feel like a cartoon villain. Their power comes from access, secrecy, and the ability to make suspicious things look like normal event logistics.

### Design

## New Orleans Fixer Crew [tentative name]

- Residence: New Orleans
- Language: English
- Playable: No
- Role: Local antagonists / Antoine’s old connections

### Backstory
The New Orleans fixer crew is made up of people connected to Antoine’s old life. They are not international masterminds. They are local operators hired to handle practical work around Jazz Fest.

They know how to move through the city, pressure vendors, get fake credentials, access service routes, and make problems disappear without attracting attention.

They approach Antoine because he knows the city, the routes, and the kinds of people who can move through major events without drawing attention. When Antoine refuses, they notice his connection to Nia and threaten that something could happen to her if he does not cooperate.

### Story Role
The fixer crew grounds the international threat in Antoine’s world.

They connect the HEARTLINE case to New Orleans street-level danger, and they force Antoine to confront the life he tried to escape.

### Design

## HEARTLINE Whistleblower [tentative name]

- Residence: Taiwan
- Language: Mandarin Chinese, English [tentative]
- Nationality: Taiwanese [tentative]
- Playable: No
- Role: Inciting figure / source of hidden evidence

### Backstory
The HEARTLINE whistleblower is someone close enough to the group to access festival equipment, travel logistics, or internal records.

They hide evidence inside HEARTLINE’s festival equipment before the Jazz Fest trip. The evidence could expose a criminal arrangement involving a Taiwan-side insider and New Orleans local fixers.

They may be a staff member, former manager, security assistant, production worker, or someone else connected to HEARTLINE’s tour.

### Story Role
The whistleblower is the reason the main threat exists. They are trying to make the truth visible, but their actions put HEARTLINE, Nia, Antoine, and others in danger.

For now, this character does not need to be fully revealed. Their identity can stay tentative until the plot needs it.
