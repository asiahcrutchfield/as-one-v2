# Character Design

These notes describe the main playable character designs for As One v2.

The overall style should feel like grounded narrative game concept art with a polished pixel-art influence. Characters should have clear silhouettes, readable colors, and designs that support their story role and gameplay.

The game has three layers of protection:

- Nia protects herself through memory, observation, courage, and Sunny.
- Antoine protects through instinct, physical action, and local knowledge.
- 陳柏安 protects through evidence, coordination, tactics, and control.

## Nia

### Core Visual Idea
Nia is a 9-year-old Black girl from New Orleans. She should look gentle, observant, and emotionally sensitive, but not helpless. Her design should communicate that she notices small things adults miss.

She is shy at first, but curious. She carries grief quietly after Annika’s death, but she still has warmth, imagination, and moments of brightness.

### Appearance
- Warm brown skin
- Large expressive brown eyes
- Soft childlike face
- Round cheeks
- Small, thoughtful expressions
- Natural Black hair
- Two large puff ponytails, braided puffs, twist puffs, or chunky curls
- Purple hair ties, beads, or small barrettes

Nia should look clearly like a child, not a teenager. Her proportions, expressions, and clothing should stay age-appropriate.

### Clothing
Nia’s outfit should feel childlike, memorable, and practical.

Possible main outfit:
- Light sky-blue dress
- Puff sleeves
- Colorful flower embroidery or printed flower motifs
- Small buttons
- Waist tie or bow
- Ruffled hem
- Scrunched white socks
- Blue sneakers

The dress connects to childhood, softness, New Orleans gardens, and Nia’s imagined paradise. The sneakers keep her grounded and believable as a kid moving through the city.

### Accessories
- Sunny in stuffed tiger form
- Small backpack or crossbody bag
- Beaded bracelet from her mother
- HEARTLINE fan item, such as a small charm, sticker, bracelet, or folded ticket/flyer
- Possible purple accents to connect her visually to Sunny and HEARTLINE

### Color Palette
- Sky blue
- Lavender / purple accents
- Warm orange flowers
- Soft green leaves
- White / cream socks
- Blue sneakers
- Warm brown skin tones
- Dark brown hair

### Expressions
Nia needs a wide emotional range.

Important expressions:
- Small shy smile
- Curious side-glance
- Quiet sadness
- Fear while trying to stay brave
- Wonder when imagining paradise
- Determination when remembering Annika
- Relief when holding Sunny
- Panic held back through humming

### Silhouette
Nia’s silhouette should be instantly readable:
- Small child body
- Large natural hair puffs
- Soft dress shape
- Sneakers
- Stuffed tiger

### Gameplay Readability
Nia should not look combat-ready. Her gameplay is about noticing, hiding, remembering, speaking, and commanding Sunny.

Her design should make the player feel protective of her, but also believe she has inner strength.

### Design Keywords
Gentle, observant, shy, curious, brave, grieving, childlike, warm, imaginative, grounded, New Orleans, flowers, memory, HEARTLINE fan.

### AI Prompt

## Sunny

Sunny has two visual forms:

1. Stuffed tiger form
2. Actual tiger / paradise guardian form

Sunny should always feel connected to Nia. He is not just a pet, mascot, weapon, or magical summon. He is Nia’s courage, protection, anger, and need for safety made visible.

The key idea is that Sunny begins as something Nia holds for comfort, then becomes something she can stand beside for courage.

---

## Sunny — Stuffed Tiger Form

### Core Visual Idea

Sunny’s stuffed form is Nia’s comfort object. He should look soft, huggable, child-safe, and loved.

He should feel like something Nia has carried, hugged, slept with, and held during hard moments. His design should feel emotionally important, not decorative.

Stuffed Sunny is what Nia holds when she needs comfort.

### Appearance

- Small plush tiger
- Rounded head
- Small round ears
- Soft orange body
- Black or charcoal stripes
- Cream muzzle
- Cream belly
- Soft paws
- Button-like eyes
- Stitched nose
- Embroidered whiskers
- Slightly worn fabric texture
- Clear striped tail
- Slightly tiger-like muzzle so he does not look too much like a teddy bear

### Accessories

- Purple ribbon or simple purple collar
- Optional small HEARTLINE charm or tag
- Possible stitched patch or repair mark
- The patch should feel like something repaired with love, not damage or injury

### Color Palette

- Warm orange
- Soft black / charcoal stripes
- Cream muzzle and belly
- Slight worn beige fabric tones
- Small purple accent from ribbon, collar, patch, or charm

### Silhouette

Sunny’s stuffed form should be simple and iconic:

- Round head
- Round body
- Stubby paws
- Striped tail
- Purple ribbon or collar
- Small enough for Nia to carry, hug, or tuck into a backpack

### Design Keywords

Soft, beloved, comforting, loyal, worn, child-safe, warm, huggable, emotionally important, repaired with love.

## Sunny — Actual Tiger / Paradise Guardian Form

### Core Visual Idea

Sunny’s tiger form is the stuffed tiger transformed through Nia’s imagination.

He should be an actual tiger, not a cartoon mascot, fantasy beast, monster, spirit animal, or Aslan-like sacred lion figure. He is powerful and protective, with a dreamlike paradise presence, but his body should still feel like a real tiger.

The wonder comes from a dangerous wild animal being peaceful with a child. This idea is inspired by Isaiah 11:8, where children are safe among dangerous creatures.

Guardian Sunny is what Nia feels when she finally has courage.

### Appearance

- Believable tiger anatomy
- Strong shoulders
- Large paws
- Thick tail
- Rich orange fur
- Deep black stripes
- Cream underside
- Golden eyes
- Noble, intelligent expression
- Calm guardian presence when not attacking
- Powerful enough to feel dangerous to threats
- Gentle enough to feel safe beside Nia

### Connection to Stuffed Sunny

Guardian Sunny should still feel connected to the stuffed form, but subtly.

Possible shared details:

- Similar forehead stripe pattern
- Similar warm orange and cream color relationship
- Similar gentle eye shape, but with real golden tiger eyes instead of button eyes
- Optional subtle purple collar, ribbon, or small tag
- Optional HEARTLINE charm only if it stays understated
- No glowing markings unless the scene specifically calls for symbolic emphasis

Avoid making the collar or charm too large, too magical, or too branded. Sunny should read first as a real tiger, then as Nia’s guardian, then as a symbol.

### Expressions / Poses

Important poses:

- Standing between Nia and danger
- Low protective stance
- Roaring to push back fear
- Calmly walking beside Nia
- Resting near Nia in paradise
- Looking directly at a threat
- Lowering himself gently near Nia
- Watching silently while Nia notices something

### Color Palette

- Rich orange
- Deep black stripes
- Cream fur
- Golden eyes
- Warm sunlight highlights
- Natural shadow tones
- Small purple accent only if needed

### Silhouette

Guardian Sunny’s silhouette should feel like a real tiger:

- Long powerful body
- Heavy shoulders
- Large paws
- Strong neck and head
- Thick tail
- Calm but alert posture

He should not look like a lion, wolf, fantasy creature, or oversized plush.

### Design Keywords

Guardian, loyal, noble, powerful, protective, real tiger, peaceful paradise, warm, courageous, companion, courage made visible.

## Antoine

### Core Visual Idea
Antoine is a 30-year-old Black man from New Orleans. He is Nia’s uncle, protective and physically capable, but emotionally guarded.

His design should sit between two worlds:
- the street-level life he is trying to escape
- the stable adult life he wants to build

He should look like someone who has been in fights before and knows how to read danger, but he should not look like a superhero or exaggerated action character.

### Appearance
- Black man, age 30
- Athletic build
- Broad shoulders
- Strong arms
- Warm brown skin
- Short locs, twists, or fade
- Trimmed beard and mustache
- Alert, guarded eyes
- Tired but controlled expression

Antoine should feel grounded and real. His strength should come from experience, not fantasy exaggeration.

### Clothing
Possible main outfit:
- Plain white or off-white T-shirt
- Fitted blue jeans
- Clean sneakers
- Light jacket, hoodie, or overshirt as optional alternate outfit
- Practical streetwear suitable for New Orleans

He should look like he could pick Nia up from family, blend into the city, or suddenly have to fight if danger appears.

### Accessories
- Gold chain
- Fleur-de-lis pendant
- Watch
- Bracelet
- Small earrings
- Forearm tattoos

The jewelry and tattoos should suggest history and personal identity, not a stereotype. Use restraint.

### Color Palette
- Off-white
- Denim blue
- Dark brown
- Black
- Muted gold
- Warm skin tones
- Optional deep green, burgundy, or navy accents

### Expressions
Important expressions:
- Calm but suspicious
- Protective focus
- Anger under control
- Guilt
- Tension when old contacts appear
- Fear when Nia is threatened
- Determination during combat

### Silhouette
Antoine’s silhouette should be strong and simple:
- Broad shoulders
- Relaxed streetwear
- Strong arms
- Sneakers
- Chain / pendant
- Tattoos

### Gameplay Readability
Antoine is the physical-action character. He should look like he can fight, grab, shove, dodge, protect, and survive.

But his design should also show restraint. His goal is protection, not violence for its own sake.

### Design Keywords
Protective uncle, street-smart, guarded, strong, restrained, New Orleans, old life, trying to change, physical, loyal, tense.

### AI Prompt

He should look grounded and believable, not like a superhero. Athletic build, broad shoulders, strong arms, alert guarded expression, trimmed beard, short locs or twists with a fade. Outfit: plain off-white T-shirt, fitted blue jeans, clean sneakers, subtle jewelry such as a gold chain or fleur-de-lis pendant, watch or bracelet, and visible forearm tattoos. He should feel protective, restrained, and dangerous only when he has to be. His design should suggest a man caught between his old street life and the better life he wants for himself and his family. Style: game character concept art, full-body pose, expression/action insets, grounded modern New Orleans streetwear.

## 陳柏安

### Core Visual Idea
陳柏安 is a 34-year-old Taiwanese police investigator and international security liaison.

He should look calm, disciplined, observant, and professional. He is tactical, but his main strength is investigation, coordination, and reading patterns.

He should not look like a generic SWAT character or action-movie super-cop.

### Appearance
- Taiwanese man, age 34
- Lean athletic build
- Short neat black hair
- Clean-shaven or very light stubble
- Sharp observant eyes
- Calm, serious expression
- Upright posture
- Controlled body language

柏安 should look like someone who notices inconsistencies immediately.

### Clothing
His design can have two modes:

#### Investigator Mode
- Dark jacket
- Collared shirt
- Slacks or practical dark pants
- Badge / ID
- Earpiece
- Notebook, phone, or tablet
- Compact radio

#### Field / Tactical Mode
- Navy police or tactical uniform
- Utility vest
- Reflective yellow-green elements
- Radio
- Gloves
- Utility belt
- Baton
- Flashlight
- Credential holder
- Optional compact shield

Because he is an international liaison, he should look prepared but not like he is trying to act outside his authority.

### Visual Identity
Use subtle Taiwanese police/investigator cues:
- Navy uniform
- Mandarin text patches
- ID badge
- Official-looking patches
- Reflective strips
- Practical communication gear

Avoid inaccurate over-militarized design unless the scene specifically calls for tactical response.

### Color Palette
- Navy
- Black
- Charcoal
- Muted gray
- Reflective yellow-green
- Small gold / blue official accents

### Expressions
Important expressions:
- Calm focus
- Suspicion
- Reading a clue
- Quiet frustration
- Tactical urgency
- Controlled concern
- Reconsidering Nia’s testimony

### Silhouette
柏安’s silhouette should be clean and precise:
- Upright stance
- Sharp uniform lines
- Utility vest or jacket
- Radio / ID / equipment
- Controlled posture

### Gameplay Readability
柏安 is the tactics/investigation character.

His design should communicate:
- scan
- mark suspect
- coordinate
- secure area
- protect civilians
- restrain
- control space

He should contrast Antoine:
- Antoine feels physical and instinctive.
- 柏安 feels precise and procedural.

### Design Keywords
Taiwanese investigator, international liaison, calm, precise, disciplined, tactical, evidence, control, professional, observant, restrained.
