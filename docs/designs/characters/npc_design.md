# NPC Design

These notes describe the major non-player character designs for As One v2.

NPCs should support the emotional and plot structure without overcomplicating the prototype. Each design should make the character’s story role clear quickly.

## Annika

### Core Visual Idea
Annika is Nia’s 9-year-old best friend. She should feel bright, outgoing, playful, and emotionally present.

She must not feel like a character who exists only to die. Her design should make it believable that Nia remembers her voice, courage, and energy after she is gone.

### Appearance
- Black girl, age 9
- Warm brown skin
- Bright expressive eyes
- Open, friendly smile
- Lively posture
- More outgoing body language than Nia

Annika should visually contrast Nia. If Nia is shy and observant, Annika is more expressive and bold.

### Hair
Possible styles:
- Braids with beads
- Twists with colorful clips
- Ponytails with bright accessories
- Braided bob
- Natural curls with headband

Her hair accessories can be brighter than Nia’s.

### Clothing
Annika’s outfit should feel childlike, practical, and slightly more energetic than Nia’s.

Possible outfit:
- Bright T-shirt or blouse
- Skirt, shorts, or overalls
- Sneakers
- HEARTLINE bracelet, pin, charm, or small fan accessory
- Colorful beads or hair clips

She should look like someone who pulls Nia into fun.

### Color Palette
- Warm yellow
- Coral
- Pink
- Purple
- Denim
- White
- Bright accent colors

### Expressions
Important expressions:
- Big smile
- Encouraging grin
- Playful teasing
- Excited HEARTLINE fan energy
- Serious “be brave” look
- Memory version: soft, comforting expression

### Story Readability
Annika should be visually memorable enough that the player understands why Nia misses her. She should feel like warmth, friendship, and courage.

After her death, imagined or remembered versions of Annika can appear softer, slightly glowing, or surrounded by HEARTLINE/paradise visual motifs. Keep this subtle.

### Design Keywords
Outgoing, brave, playful, sister-like, warm, encouraging, HEARTLINE fan, bright, remembered, beloved.

### AI Prompt
Design Annika, a 9-year-old Black girl from New Orleans for a narrative game. She is Nia’s best friend, outgoing, playful, brave, and encouraging. She should feel bright and full of life, not like a tragic symbol. Warm brown skin, expressive eyes, big smile, lively body language. Hair could be braids with beads, twists, ponytails, or natural curls with colorful accessories. Outfit should be childlike and energetic: bright top, skirt or shorts or overalls, sneakers, and a small HEARTLINE fan accessory such as a bracelet, pin, charm, or sticker. She should visually contrast Nia by feeling more confident and expressive. Style: narrative game character concept art, full-body child character design, expression studies.

## Nia’s Mom [tentative name]

### Core Visual Idea
Nia’s mother is a grieving parent trying to hold her household together through faith, caution, and love.

She should feel caring, tired, protective, and emotionally restrained. She is not cold. She is overwhelmed.

### Appearance
- Black woman, age 32–38
- Warm brown skin
- Practical hairstyle
- Calm but tired eyes
- Gentle face
- Protective posture

She should look like someone who has responsibilities and not enough rest.

### Hair
Possible styles:
- Natural hair pulled back
- Braids
- Low bun
- Short natural style
- Headwrap for home scenes

### Clothing
Her clothing can change by context.

Home:
- Comfortable blouse
- Cardigan
- Long skirt or practical pants
- House shoes or flats

Jehovah’s Witness / field service / meeting:
- Modest dress
- Cardigan or blazer
- Simple flats
- Bible bag or literature bag

Jazz Fest conflict:
- Practical outfit, worried parent energy, maybe cardigan, tote bag, phone in hand

### Accessories
- Simple earrings
- Wedding ring or absent ring depending on story choice
- Bible / meeting bag
- Phone
- Keys
- Tissue or handkerchief
- Small purse

### Color Palette
- Warm earth tones
- Soft blue
- Brown
- Cream
- Muted green
- Burgundy or plum accents

### Expressions
Important expressions:
- Gentle comfort
- Worry
- Exhaustion
- Forced calm
- Protective anger
- Religious reassurance
- Fear when Nia is in danger

### Story Readability
Nia’s mom represents adult protection and religious comfort. She believes paradise can comfort Nia, but she does not fully understand the complicated way Nia is grieving.

Her design should show love and concern, not villainy or neglect.

### Design Keywords
Mother, protective, grieving, faithful, tired, warm, modest, cautious, loving, overwhelmed.

### AI Prompt
Design Nia’s mother for a narrative game. She is a Black woman in her early-to-mid 30s from New Orleans, Nia’s mother and Antoine’s sister. She is loving, protective, tired, and grieving, trying to comfort her daughter through faith after Annika’s death. Warm brown skin, practical hairstyle such as braids, low bun, natural hair, or headwrap. Clothing should be modest and grounded: cardigan, blouse, long skirt or practical pants, simple flats, possible Bible or meeting bag. She should look caring but exhausted, protective but not harsh. Style: grounded narrative game NPC concept art, full-body design, expression studies.

## Antoine’s Girlfriend [tentative name]

### Core Visual Idea
Antoine’s girlfriend is emotionally perceptive and grounded. She supports Antoine, but she is not there to save him from himself.

She should look like a real adult woman with a strong sense of self. She can be stylish, but not overly glamorous unless the story later calls for it.

### Appearance
- Black woman, age 28–32
- Confident posture
- Observant eyes
- Warm but direct expression
- Grounded adult presence

She should look like someone who can read Antoine’s mood before he says anything.

### Hair
Possible styles:
- Braids
- Locs
- Natural curls
- Sleek ponytail
- Short natural cut

### Clothing
Possible outfit:
- Fitted jeans or trousers
- Simple top
- Jacket, cardigan, or blazer
- Sneakers, boots, or flats
- Practical but stylish New Orleans casualwear

She should feel like she has her own life, not just an accessory to Antoine.

### Accessories
- Hoop earrings
- Necklace
- Watch
- Small purse
- Phone
- Work badge, keys, or planner if she has a job-focused design

### Color Palette
- Deep green
- Cream
- Brown
- Denim
- Gold accents
- Rust / burnt orange
- Black

### Expressions
Important expressions:
- Loving but concerned
- “I know you’re hiding something”
- Frustrated
- Calm boundary-setting
- Fear when things escalate
- Relief when Antoine chooses honesty

### Story Readability
She challenges Antoine’s belief that protection means carrying everything alone. She should visually represent the future Antoine wants, but also the future he could lose if he keeps hiding things.

### Design Keywords
Grounded, perceptive, stylish, warm, direct, boundary-setting, adult, supportive, not naïve.

## HEARTLINE

### Core Visual Idea
HEARTLINE is an internationally famous Taiwanese pop act. For the current scope, HEARTLINE should function as a believable pop act and emotional anchor, not a fully overbuilt idol group.

The group can remain visually flexible until the story needs individual members.

### Public Image
HEARTLINE should feel:
- polished
- bright
- emotionally sincere
- internationally marketable
- clean but not childish
- suitable for Jazz Fest posters
- believable as a Taiwanese pop act with global appeal

### Visual Motifs
- Hearts
- lines / connection
- sound waves
- stage lights
- soft neon
- sky blue / purple / white / silver
- festival posters
- friendship bracelets
- fan charms
- logo stickers
- official lanyards

### Color Palette
- Purple
- Sky blue
- White
- Silver
- Soft pink
- Black for contrast
- Gold accents for stage branding

### Logo Ideas
The HEARTLINE logo should be simple enough for Nia to recognize on:
- posters
- badges
- stickers
- merchandise
- tour equipment
- lanyards

Possible logo concepts:
- heart connected to a waveform
- heartbeat line crossing through the word HEARTLINE
- stylized H/L monogram
- heart-shaped stage light
- line connecting two small stars

### Story Readability
HEARTLINE must be recognizable enough that Nia can notice details adults miss.

Important prop variations:
- current official logo
- outdated logo
- fake badge logo
- tour crate sticker
- fan bracelet
- concert flyer
- stage banner

### Scope Note
Do not fully design every member yet unless needed. For the prototype, focus on:
- logo
- poster
- fan merch
- one song motif
- one equipment crate
- one official badge/lanyard

### AI Prompt
Design branding for HEARTLINE, an internationally famous Taiwanese pop act in a narrative game. HEARTLINE is the emotional anchor for two 9-year-old fans and the public-facing center of a hidden thriller plot at New Orleans Jazz Fest. The branding should feel polished, bright, sincere, internationally marketable, and believable as a Taiwanese pop act. Use motifs of hearts, connection lines, sound waves, stage lights, sky blue, purple, white, silver, and soft neon. Create a simple readable logo that could appear on posters, fan bracelets, lanyards, tour crates, badges, stickers, and stage banners. Do not overdesign individual members. Focus on logo, poster, fan merch, and official tour materials. Style: game worldbuilding concept art, music group branding sheet.

## New Orleans Fixer Crew [tentative name]

### Core Visual Idea
The New Orleans fixer crew is made of local operators connected to Antoine’s old life.

They are not cartoon gangsters. They should feel grounded, practical, and dangerous because they know the city, people, routes, and pressure points.

### Group Design Rules
The crew should look like people who can blend into:
- service routes
- vendor areas
- backstage spaces
- parking lots
- street corners
- delivery zones
- festival crowds

Their designs should avoid exaggerated gang stereotypes.

### Visual Motifs
- Workwear
- Service uniforms
- Event staff clothing
- Vendor badges
- Delivery jackets
- Hoodies
- Dark jeans
- Gloves
- Caps
- Practical shoes
- Fake credentials

### Color Palette
- Black
- Dark gray
- Navy
- Brown
- Faded denim
- Safety orange or yellow if disguised as workers
- Muted red or green accents

### Crew Archetypes
Possible members:

#### Fixer Lead
Older, calm, intimidating, knows Antoine personally. Does not need to yell. Threatens through certainty.

#### Access Man
Handles badges, vendor routes, forged lanyards, equipment schedules.

#### Driver / Courier
Moves people or crates. Knows back streets, loading docks, and service entrances.

#### Muscle
Physical threat. Not flashy. Practical clothing, heavy build, controlled violence.

### Story Readability
The crew should make Antoine uncomfortable because he recognizes them. They should feel like the past walking back into his life.

Their threat to Nia should feel personal and ugly, not theatrical.

### Design Keywords
Local, grounded, practical, intimidating, service routes, fake badges, old life, pressure, quiet threat, New Orleans.

### AI Prompt
Design a grounded New Orleans fixer crew for a narrative action thriller game. They are local operators connected to Antoine’s old life and hired to exploit Jazz Fest access routes. They are not cartoon gangsters or international masterminds. They should look practical, dangerous, and able to blend into service areas, vendor routes, parking lots, backstage spaces, and festival crowds. Use workwear, event staff clothing, delivery jackets, hoodies, dark jeans, gloves, caps, practical shoes, and fake credentials. Include several archetypes: calm fixer lead, badge/access man, driver/courier, and muscle. Style: grounded game NPC faction concept art, modern New Orleans, realistic street-level danger.
